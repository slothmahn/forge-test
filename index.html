Foundry cbBTC Swap Limit

Maximum ETH in each cbBTC conversion swap. Failed large swaps retry at smaller sizes within the same mint transaction. Each swap checks a fresh quote and minimum output; a conversion that cannot finish safely reverts.

05 · YOUR NFTS

Your Forge Positions

Browse your active Forge positions. Switch to Ended when you want to review your position history.

Connect a test wallet to see your positions.

The share shown is a live estimate. Final reward shares use the power snapshot at each pool’s deadline. Transferring an NFT also transfers future and unclaimed rewards to the new owner. Principal stays whole during the grace period, then the on-screen decay estimate updates each second; the actual amount returned uses the transaction’s block time. Maturity and decay do not close the NFT automatically; ending or finalizing it requires a transaction.

Fully decayed positions appear here while awaiting an on-chain burn; their NFTs are still live until anyone finalizes them. Completed positions show the principal actually returned, and previously earned rewards remain claimable by the final owner.
